What's the difference between VIANUscrubs and a regular insole?
+
Regular insoles are made for a standardized foot shape — which is a statistical average that fits almost nobody well. VIANUscrubs is built from photos of your actual foot: your arch height, your pressure distribution, your geometry. It goes where your stock insole was. Same fit, completely different result.
How does the 3-photo scan actually work?
+
You take three photos with any smartphone — 45° from the inside arch, 45° from the outside, and top-down on your existing insole on a flat surface. Our AI extracts your foot geometry from these images, cross-references against biomechanical models validated by orthopedic master shoemakers, and generates your production file. The whole thing takes about 2 minutes.
Will it fit in my nursing clogs / Danskos / Hokas?
+
VIANUscrubs is designed with a slim profile specifically for clinical footwear. It fits in the vast majority of shoes that have a removable stock insole — including Dansko, Hoka, New Balance, Brooks, Alegria, and most nursing clog brands. When you complete your scan, we confirm compatibility for your specific shoe model before production.
How quickly will I notice a difference?
+
Most customers notice a difference within the first shift — particularly in forefoot pressure and arch support. Knee and hip relief typically builds over 1–2 weeks as your gait adjusts to proper support. If you don't notice meaningful improvement within 30 days, we'll refund you in full.
Can I really use it in multiple shoes?
+
Yes — that's the point. VIANUscrubs replaces the removable stock insole in any shoe. Pull it out of your nursing clogs, drop it into your running shoes for the gym, switch it back. It takes about five seconds. One footbed covers your whole rotation rather than being locked to a single pair.
What happens after 3 years?
+
We'll remind you and arrange collection of your worn pair. We analyse the wear patterns — which improves the AI for future customers — and recycle the TPU material. You get a new custom pair at a loyalty price. The whole model is designed to be circular from day one.
Is this covered by insurance or HSA/FSA?
+
VIANUscrubs is FSA/HSA eligible in most cases as a custom orthopedic product. We provide a detailed receipt for submission. Insurance reimbursement varies by plan — check with your provider. We're working on direct insurance billing partnerships and will announce updates via our newsletter.
