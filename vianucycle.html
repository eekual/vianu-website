A professional cyclist flexes their knee up to 5 million times per season. Every single stroke starts at the foot. Yet the stock insoles in most cycling shoes are flat foam — designed for a warehouse, not a peloton. Misalignment at the foot migrates upstream: hot spots, knee tracking errors, hip rocking, back strain. A perfect bike fit built on an imperfect foundation.

Maximum Power Transfer
Arch collapse loses watts every stroke. A rigid custom insole maintains foot geometry under load — more of your effort reaches the pedal.

Knee Tracking Precision
Custom arch support corrects tibial rotation and reduces frontal-plane knee drift — critical for GC riders covering 3,000+ km in three weeks.

Metatarsal pressure is distributed evenly across the forefoot — eliminating the burning and numbness that forces criterium riders to back off in the final laps.

Delayed Fatigue
Stabilised foot mechanics reduce compensatory muscle recruitment up the chain. Less wasted energy = more left for the final climb.
🏛
Stability Across Terrain
Variable surfaces demand constant micro-corrections. A custom insole keeps the foot neutral regardless of what the trail throws at it.

Long-Haul Comfort
